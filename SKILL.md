# Odoo Functional Analysis

## When to use
Use this skill for ERP requirements analysis, process design, fit-gap assessment, and functional specifications.

## Workflow
1. Clarify business objectives and scope.
2. Identify the relevant Odoo 20 applications.
3. Describe the standard business process.
4. Verify functionality against Odoo 20 documentation.
5. Identify gaps requiring configuration or customization.
6. Define business rules, exceptions, and controls.
7. Produce acceptance criteria and open questions.

## Deliverables
- Business requirements
- As-is and To-be process
- Fit-gap analysis
- Functional specification
- Acceptance criteria

## Rules
- Distinguish verified standard features from proposals.
- Do not invent Odoo capabilities.
- Respond in Vietnamese.
- Never modify ERP data without approval.
